# Membership

## Trail Club points

Trail Club is our free membership. Members collect points on purchases, rentals and repair
fees and get early access to the spring and autumn sales. Joining takes a minute online or at
any till; you only need an email address and a postcode. Points are tied to the member, not to
a card, so purchases in store and online both count.

## Earning points

You earn 1 point for every 1 spent on full-price gear and 2 points for every 1 spent on
rentals. Bringing your own bag to a store earns 5 bonus points per visit. Points from an order
appear 14 days after delivery, once the return window for that order is underway, and are
removed if you return the items.

## Redeeming points

Every 500 points can be turned into a voucher worth 10. Vouchers are valid for 12 months and
can be combined with each other but not with discount codes. You can redeem points at checkout
online or ask a store colleague to scan your membership barcode.

## Leaving the club

You can leave Trail Club at any time from your profile page. Unused points expire on the day
you leave and cannot be paid out in cash. If you rejoin later, you start again with zero
points, but your order history is kept in your customer account.
