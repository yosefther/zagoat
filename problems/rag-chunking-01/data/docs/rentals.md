# Rentals

## Rental periods

Tents, packs, stoves and snowshoes can be rented for a weekend, which runs from Friday noon to
Monday noon, or for a full week of 7 nights. Bookings open 90 days ahead. Collect from any
store or choose home delivery for a fee of 9. Rentals cannot be extended once they have
started if another customer has booked the same item.

## Deposits

Every rental needs a refundable deposit held on a credit card: 50 for small items such as
stoves and headlamps, and 150 for tents and packs. The hold is released within 2 working days
after the gear is checked back in. We cannot accept debit cards or cash for deposits.

## Late returns

Gear returned after noon on the due date is charged as one extra day at the daily rate. After
3 days without contact we treat the gear as lost and charge the full replacement price from
the deposit card. If you are running late because of weather or a road closure, call the
store before the due time and we waive the late charge once per year.

## Damage during a rental

Small wear such as dirt, scuffs and a bent peg is expected and free. Torn fabric, burns from a
stove, or a broken pole are charged at repair cost, capped at the deposit amount. Tell us about
damage when you return the gear rather than hiding it; honest reports are charged at half the
repair cost.
