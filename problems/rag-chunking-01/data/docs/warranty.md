# Warranty

## What the warranty covers

Every Fernhollow-branded product carries a 3-year warranty against defects in materials and
workmanship. That includes delaminating seams, failed zips, snapped tent poles under normal
load and soles that separate from boots. The warranty belongs to the original buyer and
needs a proof of purchase such as an order number or receipt.

## What the warranty excludes

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from accidents, fire, rodents or
misuse, or gear that someone other than us has modified or repaired. Fading from sunlight,
pilling of fleece and the gradual loss of waterproof coating are expected with use. Rental
gear and items bought second hand are not covered.

## Making a warranty claim

Start a claim from your order history and describe the fault in a few sentences. Attach two
photos: one of the whole item and one close-up of the defect. Our repair team replies within
4 working days with a prepaid shipping label if they need to see the item. Please wash and dry
gear before sending it in.

## Repairs and replacements

We repair first, because a fixed jacket keeps its story and stays out of landfill. Typical
repairs take 2 to 3 weeks, and we return the item free of charge. If a repair is not possible,
we replace the item with the same model or the closest current equivalent. If no equivalent
exists, we issue store credit for the original price.
