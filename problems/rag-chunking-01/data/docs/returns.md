# Returns

## Return window

You can return most gear within 45 days of delivery. The window starts on the date the
carrier marks the parcel as delivered, not the order date. Gifts follow the same window,
counted from the delivery to the person who placed the order. If the 45th day falls on a
public holiday, the window closes on the next working day. Returns started after the window
closes are refused at the warehouse and shipped back to the sender at their own cost.

## Condition of returned items

Returned gear must be clean, dry and unused outdoors. Tents must be packed in their original
stuff sack with every pole, peg and guy line. Boots must show no wear on the soles. Sleeping
bags must not smell of smoke. Items with removed tags or worn soles are inspected by hand and
may be refunded at a reduced rate, between 50 and 80 percent of the price paid, depending on
their state.

## Refund timing

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once the warehouse scans your parcel, the
refund is issued within 5 working days. Card issuers can take another 3 to 10 days to show
the credit on a statement. Store credit refunds appear in your account immediately after the
scan. We email a receipt with the refunded amount for every refund.

## Exchanges

To swap a size or colour, start an exchange instead of a return. We reserve the replacement
for 14 days while your original item travels back to us. Exchanges ship free once, in each
direction. If the replacement costs more, we charge the difference when it ships; if it costs
less, we refund the difference to your card.

## Items that cannot be returned

Some items are final sale: opened fuel canisters, water filters with a used cartridge,
personalised embroidery, and anything bought in the clearance outlet marked as final. Gift
cards cannot be returned or exchanged for cash. Safety gear such as climbing harnesses and
helmets cannot be returned once the hang tag has been cut.
