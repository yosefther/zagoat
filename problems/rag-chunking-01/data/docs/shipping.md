# Shipping

## Processing time

Orders placed before 2 pm on a working day leave our warehouse the same day. Orders placed
later, or on weekends, leave the next working day. Pre-order items ship when stock arrives,
and we email a dispatch notice with a tracking link the moment the label is printed. During
the November sale, allow one extra working day for packing.

## Shipping rates

Standard delivery costs 4.95 and takes 3 to 5 working days. Express delivery costs 12.50 and
arrives in 1 to 2 working days. Orders over 75 qualify for free standard delivery. Bulky
items such as kayaks and roof boxes carry a flat oversize surcharge of 30, even when the rest
of the order ships free.

## International orders

We ship to 31 countries in Europe and to Canada. Customs duties and import taxes are charged
by the destination country and paid by the recipient on delivery. Lithium batteries and fuel
canisters cannot be shipped abroad because of air freight rules. International parcels take
6 to 12 working days and always include tracking.

## Lost or damaged parcels

If tracking has not moved for 7 days, contact us and we open an investigation with the
carrier. If a parcel arrives crushed or wet, photograph the box and the contents before
unpacking further and send the photos within 48 hours. We send a replacement as soon as the
carrier confirms the loss or damage, usually within 10 days.
